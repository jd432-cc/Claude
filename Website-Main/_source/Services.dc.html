Data & Telemetry

We instrument the car to answer specific questions, not to fill a dashboard. Math channels turn raw traces into decisions: load transfer, damper work, tyre slip and the cost of driving style.

Debrief

02

ECU Calibration

Peak power doesn't win events; area under the curve and repeatability do. We calibrate for real load, real temperature and the fuel actually available at the event.

  • Dyno development with load cell and thermal control
  • Restrictor, BoP and technical-regulation compliance
  • Anti-lag, launch control, safety strategies
  • Surface and stage maps, flashed on site

The loop

How a programme moves, week to week.

STEP 01

Frame

Season target, budget, rulebook. We decide what the clock must gain, and where.

STEP 02

Baseline

Dyno, shakedown and a data session. Nothing changes before there's a measured starting point.

STEP 03

Develop

One variable at a time, documented. Gains are kept, the rest is thrown out.

STEP 04

Operate

Event run, report delivered, loop reopened for the next outing.
